## Notes

- Items marked incomplete require spec updates before `/speckit-clarify` or `/speckit-plan`
- Validated in one pass on 2026-10-08; all items pass.
- The spec is authored as parts in `spec-src/`; `spec.md` is generated by
  `scripts/build-spec.sh` and must not be edited directly. CLI contracts are in
  `contracts/`, one file per command group.
- Content moved here from `specs/001-research-workspace`: the methodology half of User Story
  7 (FR-039). That story keeps datasets and carries a note; its `cli-method.md` contract is
  replaced by the one here.
- "All the meta parts of a research that in general is implicit" is the least bounded phrase
  of any request so far. It is read as five kinds of record — conventions, methodologies,
  assumptions, decisions (with lessons), and checklists — plus two mechanisms: showing what
  applies at the moment of work, and recording departures. Anything else implicit that the
  requester had in mind is not covered and should be raised with `/speckit-clarify`.
- The request named origins for conventions (lab, community) and for methodologies
  (research, lab, community, literature). The spec uses one list for everything and adds
  institution, venue, and funder, since rules from those sources behave differently (the
  researcher cannot waive them).
- No [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers were used. Choices to confirm:
  - The tool records and shows conventions; it does not check whether they were followed.
  - Precedence between conflicting conventions follows who can waive them (FR-020). This is
    a judgement and the part most open to disagreement.
  - No community guideline or handbook is shipped, because of licences.
  - A handbook is shared as a file, not live.
- User Stories 4 (assumptions, decisions, lessons) and 5 (checklists) are separable from the
  rest and could each be their own spec if this one proves too large to plan.
- A handle-prefix collision was found and resolved in the contracts: `dep` already means a
  deposit (`specs/002-research-lifecycle`), so a departure is `dpt`.
- SC-010 (a newcomer finds what applies in under two minutes without asking) needs a test
  with real newcomers.
