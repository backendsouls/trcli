## Notes

- Items marked incomplete require spec updates before `/speckit-clarify` or `/speckit-plan`
- Validated in one pass on 2026-10-08; all items pass.
- The spec is authored as parts in `spec-src/`; `spec.md` is generated by
  `scripts/build-spec.sh` and must not be edited directly. CLI contracts are in
  `contracts/`, one file per command group.
- Content moved here from `specs/001-research-workspace` (User Story 2, FR-058 to FR-064),
  which keeps a pointer; its story and requirement numbers are unchanged. Its
  `cli-question.md` contract moved here and gained `question quiet` and `question history`.
- "So that it is not forgotten" is read as three mechanisms: capture that asks nothing
  (User Story 1), an inbox that is sorted (User Story 2), and a review that brings things
  back (User Story 5). These are additions the request implies but does not spell out.
- No [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers were used. Choices to confirm with `/speckit-clarify`:
  - A "thought" (raw, in the inbox) is distinct from an "idea" (kept). This is what allows
    capture to ask nothing.
  - Topics are records with a hierarchy and a page; tags stay as plain labels; glossary
    concepts stay in `specs/002-research-lifecycle`.
  - Review periods default to 3 months (ideas), 2 (questions), 6 (hypotheses).
  - The tool shows what is due when it is used; it sends no notifications.
- User Story 6 (developing an idea: arguments, ratings, states, credit) is the most
  optional part and the first to cut.
- A naming collision was found and resolved in the contracts: `trcli review` already means
  a literature review, so the review of ideas is `trcli review-ideas` (also `revisit`).
- SC-010 (no unsorted thought older than 45 days for a user who reviews monthly) describes a
  habit, and can only be observed with users.
- FR-012 (similar ideas shown at sorting) is by shared wording only; the assumptions say so.
