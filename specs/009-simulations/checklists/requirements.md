## Notes

- Items marked incomplete require spec updates before `/speckit-clarify` or `/speckit-plan`
- Validated in one pass on 2026-10-08; all items pass.
- The spec is authored as parts in `spec-src/`; `spec.md` is generated by
  `scripts/build-spec.sh` and must not be edited directly. CLI contracts are in
  `contracts/`, one file per command group.
- "As part of experiment but different spec" is read as: a simulation **is** an experiment
  of the kind "simulation" (`specs/004-experiments`); this spec only adds to it (FR-050,
  SC-012). `specs/004-experiments` carries a one-line pointer and is otherwise unchanged.
- No [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers were used. Choices to confirm with `/speckit-clarify`:
  - TRCLI does not simulate; the researcher's simulator does, driven by the pipeline.
  - A scenario is the simulation form of a condition; a replication is a run with a seed.
  - Statistics go one step beyond 004: an interval for the mean and paired comparisons.
  - Sensitivity analysis is one input at a time; calibration and optimization are out.
- "Written for non-technical stakeholders" passes with a caveat: the audience is
  researchers who simulate, and terms such as seed, replication, warm-up, and horizon are
  theirs; each is explained where first used.
- SC-003 (identical outputs on rerun) depends on the researcher's simulator honouring its
  seed; the repeatability check exists to detect when it does not.
- SC-007 (statistics agree with an independent calculation) needs reference calculations
  prepared at planning time.
- How a simulator receives inputs and seed and reports outputs is deliberately left to
  planning; `contracts/cli-sim.md` states what is exchanged, not how.
