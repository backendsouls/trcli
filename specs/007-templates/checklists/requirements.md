## Notes

- Items marked incomplete require spec updates before `/speckit-clarify` or `/speckit-plan`
- Validated in one pass on 2026-10-08; all items pass.
- The spec is authored as parts in `spec-src/`; `spec.md` is generated by
  `scripts/build-spec.sh` and must not be edited directly. CLI contracts are in
  `contracts/`, one file per command group.
- No [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] markers were used. Scope choices to confirm with
  `/speckit-clarify`:
  - Templates are for **documents** (papers, theses, reports, letters, notes). Reusable
    shapes for records (project milestones, pipelines, report definitions) keep their own
    names in their own specs.
  - Documents are plain text in a writing format the researcher already uses; the tool does
    not typeset or produce word-processor files. Which formats is left to planning.
  - Publishers' and institutions' official templates are not shipped; users bring them in.
- New content beyond "templates exist": managed parts refreshed from the workspace (User
  Story 2), checking a document against its template, including word limits and author
  anonymity (User Story 6), three levels with precedence, and template packs for sharing.
- "Written for non-technical stakeholders" passes with a caveat: "placeholder", "managed
  part", and "writing format" are unavoidable terms, each defined where first used.
- FR-031 and SC-006 (a template cannot run commands or read outside the workspace) are
  security requirements and deserve explicit tests at planning time.
- This spec touches documents owned by 001, 002, 004, 005, and 006. It adds a
  `--template` option to their document-producing commands and leaves their content rules
  untouched (FR-035). SC-007 guards that nothing changes when no template is chosen.
- SC-011 (share of papers started from a template) can only be measured with users.
